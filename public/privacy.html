NetGuard AI · Privacy

What we store

Your email, your name, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, the names of your devices, the alerts and logs NetGuard creates, and the IP addresses it saw attacking you. API keys for devices are stored only as hashes.

What we never do

We never sell or share your data. We never see your password. Other users cannot see your data. The site owner can see how many accounts exist and each account's email, but not your alerts or logs.

AI Copilot

If the owner turned on the real AI, your questions and a short summary of your security (alert types, attacker IPs, device names and counts) are sent to the AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic) to write the answer. Passwords, emails and API keys are never sent. If no AI key is set, nothing leaves the server.

Map and location lookups

The threat map loads map pictures (tiles) from OpenStreetMap, or from Esri if OpenStreetMap is unavailable. Your browser contacts them for this, so they can see your IP address and which part of the map you are viewing. To place an attacker on the map, our server (not your browser) sends that attacker's public IP address to the free ipwho.is lookup service. Private and internal addresses are never sent.

E-mail verification

If the owner turned it on, a one-time code is sent to your e-mail address through the owner's mail provider when you create an account. Sign-ups from fake or disposable e-mail domains are refused.

Cookies

One sign-in cookie, only to keep you logged in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Your control

You can delete your account and all its data at any time in Settings. If you forget your password, ask the site owner for a one-hour reset link.
